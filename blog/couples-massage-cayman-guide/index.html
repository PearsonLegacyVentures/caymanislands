Two therapists is simple. Two therapists plus two tables plus a tiny hotel room is geometry. Plan the space first.

Allow room for two tables

Couples massage works best in villas, suites, residences and rooms with enough open floor area for two tables and safe therapist movement.

You do not need the same pressure

One guest can request Swedish-style relaxation while the other asks for firmer work. Both treatments can run together.
