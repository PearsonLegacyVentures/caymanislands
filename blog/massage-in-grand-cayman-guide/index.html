1. Choose the treatment

Swedish is the relaxation-first option. Deep Tissue is firmer and more targeted. Couples Massage brings two therapists so two guests can be treated at the same time.

2. Choose 60 or 90 minutes

Current rates are Swedish $145 / $185, Deep Tissue $165 / $220 and Couples $290 / $395.

3. Tell us exactly where you are

Property names matter. “Seven Mile Beach” is useful; the actual hotel, condo or villa is better. Access rules differ and we do not treat the appointment as confirmed until therapist availability and property access are verified.

4. We bring the treatment setup

Your therapist arrives with a professional table, linens, oils and essential supplies. You provide a clear indoor or property-approved treatment area.

5. Keep the schedule light afterward

The biggest advantage of mobile massage is what happens after: nothing. No lobby. No drive. No searching for parking while your nervous system files a complaint.
