Private massage planning for Rum Point

Property and provider access

Provide the exact address, driveway, guest-contact details and where an indoor massage table can be placed. Public waterfront space is not a confirmed private venue.

Swedish, deep tissue or couples appointments can be requested for an approved residence, but do not assume that all service durations or staffing are available at this end of the island.
